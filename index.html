BEFORE WE TALK

About your
MDS project.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

We begin by reviewing your website, CRM and booking tools. Then we recommend the connections that fit your practice and the scope we agree on.

Can our team review requests before booking?

Yes. The flow can include a staff review before calendar access, so your team can confirm the service, provider or appointment type first.

Will the design reflect our specialty?

Yes. The visual direction, service content, intake questions and next steps are tailored to your practice and your patients.

What happens after the practice review?

We discuss the priorities, confirm the work involved and agree on a plan before starting the build.

Can we start with just a website or GHL setup?

Yes. The project can focus on your website, a landing page, CRM configuration or a specific booking workflow. Content and video support can be scoped separately.

Is this a medical records system?

No. This offer covers marketing websites and business inquiry workflows, not an electronic medical records system. Data requirements, access and any necessary platform configuration must be agreed before connecting a live practice.
